Privacy

This page describes information handled by this website's contact, scheduling, and assistant features.

Contact and scheduling

If you contact us, we receive the name, email address, and business inquiry you provide so we can respond. Contact requests may be processed through FormSubmit and our website infrastructure. Scheduling is provided by Cal.com and is subject to Cal.com's privacy policy.

AI concierge

Messages sent to the AI concierge are processed by an AI service to generate a response. Please do not include passwords, financial account details, sensitive personal information, or confidential client records. An AI response may be inaccurate; confirm consequential information with us directly.

Browser storage

This site uses browser storage for interface preferences and visit context. You can clear this information through your browser settings. The capability illustrations use temporary sample data; interacting with them does not book appointments, publish reviews, or update a business system.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you have shared, or to request correction or deletion, contact hello@sentientpartners.ai.
