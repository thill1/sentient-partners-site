# Eight Contained Capability Cards

Design principle: a visually consistent 4×2 desktop gallery of elegant miniature tools, not eight large marketing panels. Each card includes one clear title, a business outcome sentence, and a demo surface. On mobile, show one or two columns depending on actual legibility. **Any sample data must be obviously illustrative.**

1. **AI Chat Assistants**: small chat transcript, prompt chips or typed input, concise response; realistic lead qualification. Use the site's existing working chat integration where safely reusable; otherwise explicitly mark as a demo.
2. **AI Voice Agents**: voice UI with consent-based start/stop, captioned transcript, book/route example; no autoplay audio; only claim live voice when functional.
3. **Websites & SEO**: miniature browser viewport of a fictional Northern California home service website, with working tab or viewport toggle; no client assets.
4. **Custom Business Apps**: miniature synthetic FieldOS-like workspace for customer, estimate, invoice, schedule tabs; show workflow concept, no client data or copied branding.
5. **CRM & Lead Management**: selectable customer pipeline, stage transitions, synthetic customer records, simple counter updates.
6. **Workflow Automation**: step-based animation or click-to-advance lead capture → follow-up → schedule → notification; motion subtle.
7. **Marketing & Analytics**: a simple campaign performance micro dashboard with selectable campaigns and synthetic metrics labeled "Illustrative demo"; avoid fictitious ROI promises.
8. **Reputation Management**: sample review request and editable draft reply, confirmation only within demo state; never simulate publishing externally.

**Strategic advisory** remains a prominent offer outside these eight technology cards, in the four-pillar architecture and a dedicated consultation path. The card selection does not constrain broader firm services such as financial readiness, business launch, document systems, and QuickBooks integrations.

Performance guidance: only active card uses rich resources, isolate demo state, lazy-load heavy dependencies and defer audio/network activation until explicit action. Make interactions keyboard accessible, with predictable controls and understandable empty states.
