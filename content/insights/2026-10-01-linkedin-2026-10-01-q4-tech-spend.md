Citizens' Q4 Business Pulse put a number on how owners are spending as the year closes.

Fifty-four percent expect revenue to rise in the next three months — the strongest outlook of 2026. Thirty percent plan to spend more on technology, including AI, up from 23% last quarter.

Among shops already using AI across several functions, 94% have cut or dropped at least one outside service. Marketing, bookkeeping, and reporting lead that list.

That pattern looks clean on a budget sheet. It gets expensive when the tool arrives and the person who used to help decide what it should carry is already gone.
