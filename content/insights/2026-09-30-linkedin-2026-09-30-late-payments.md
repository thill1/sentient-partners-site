QuickBooks’ 2026 late-payments report put a number on something owners already feel.

Fifty-nine percent of small businesses have invoices sitting 30 days or more past due. Up from 47% a year ago. The average balance waiting: about $17,700.

Revenue on the books is not cash you can use. Nearly two in five owners said one late payment made it hard to cover payroll or bills last year.

That is not mainly a sales problem. It is a timing problem between finished work and collected money.

Map when the invoice leaves, who follows up, and what happens before day 30. Then build around that rhythm.
