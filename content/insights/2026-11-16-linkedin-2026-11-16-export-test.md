Could you get your customer list out of your software today, in a file you could open somewhere else?

Most owners assume yes. Fewer have tried.

Over the years, more of the business moves into other people's systems. Customer names and phone numbers live in the booking app. Invoices and payment history live in the accounting software. Quotes, photos and signed paperwork sit in a shared drive someone set up years ago.

That's usually fine. Good tools save real time. The trouble shows up when a vendor raises prices, gets bought, drops a feature, or you simply want to switch. That's when you find out whether the records are yours in practice or only on paper.

Pick a quiet afternoon this month and run the test.

Export your customer list, your open invoices, and your job or order history.

Open each file on a computer that doesn't have the software on it.

Check that the columns you care about came with it: names, phone numbers, emails, amounts, dates.

Save a copy somewhere you control, and put a reminder on the calendar to do it again next year.

If something won't come out cleanly, it's better to learn that on a slow afternoon than in the middle of a switch.
