Another login rarely fixes the week.

Most owners already have enough software. What they don't have is one place that covers the work without five tabs and five passwords.

inTandem's 2026 digital adoption report asked 500 U.S. service-based small businesses about that friction. Forty-three percent said they would pay more for a solution that cut the total number of tools they use. Thirty-five percent switched tools in the past year. The top reasons were missing features and the need for something that does more in one place.

That is not a request for another vendor. It is a request for less toggling.

Before you add the next app, write down what you already pay for and which jobs still live in a spreadsheet or a sticky note. Keep what earns its seat. Cut what only creates another login.
