When did you last ask a good customer why they keep coming back?

Most owners guess. The website says "quality service" and "competitive prices," because that's what every website says.

Your customers usually have a much more specific answer. You call back the same day. They never have to explain the job twice. You told them once when they didn't need the expensive option, and they remembered.

This week, call three of your best customers. Not a survey, just a five-minute conversation. Ask:
Why did you pick us the first time?
What would you miss if we closed?
What almost made you go somewhere else?

Write down their exact words, not your summary of them.

Then put those words where new customers will see them: your website, your quotes, the way your team answers the phone. The third answer tells you what to fix.
