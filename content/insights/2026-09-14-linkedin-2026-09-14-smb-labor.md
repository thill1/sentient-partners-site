NFIB’s August numbers landed today.

Labor quality is still the top problem for small business owners. Twenty-three percent named it. Among owners who are hiring, 82% said they had few or no qualified applicants.

That is not mainly a “post more jobs” problem. It is a coverage problem. Missed calls, slow follow-up, and the work only a person should do get stuffed into the same hire request.
