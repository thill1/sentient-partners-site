Meta just made it a few clicks to hand an AI agent your books.

Muse for Small Business launched last week. It connects to QuickBooks, Shopify, Stripe, Slack, and your Facebook and Instagram accounts. Meta's own examples include reviewing the month's numbers for unusual expenses and drafting next week's campaign. Meta says it's free for most of what people need.

Connecting it is the easy part. Meta says nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval. That makes approval the job.

If every approval waits on the owner, the owner is still the bottleneck. If approval turns into a reflex tap, nobody is checking.

Before you connect anything, write down three things. What it can see. What it can do on its own. Who reviews the rest, and how fast.

That page matters more than the connector list.
